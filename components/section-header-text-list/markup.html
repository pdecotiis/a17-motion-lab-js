Our Values

Honest thinking, shared openly

Intellectual Humility

Staying humble allows us to question our assumptions and to update our views based on new arguments. Being skeptical and asking “why” are critical. We talk openly about mistakes because that’s the best way to learn from them.

Responsible Risk Management

At Jane Street we specialize in managing risk. We carefully study how trading risks might be bigger or more interrelated than they appear, and this allows us to be especially bold. Our risk thinking has an ethical as well as a practical dimension. We do not believe in taking excessive risk knowing that others will bear the downside. When systems fail, even when the consequences are minor, we have a strong culture of postmortems: we believe that process improvement works better than apportioning blame.

Ethics and Honesty

Jane Street looks for people who are naturally drawn to understanding how systems work. Whether it’s exploring why markets behave as they do or solving puzzles and strategy games, curiosity is key. Many successful recruits come from diverse fields like math, physics, computer science, or philosophy, not just economics.

Less hierarchy, more collaboration

We strive to minimize red tape and don’t put much stock in seniority. It’s easy to connect with colleagues no matter their department. People who work here are partners working together toward shared goals.
